About this sample. A design and engineering study for breadth, sector-rotation and relative-strength screens. All market data is simulated from a seeded random walk so the page runs on its own; the metrics, structure and interactions mirror a production analytics view.
Design decisions: screens grouped by the question a trader asks · key numbers first, detail one click away · one diverging gain/loss scale everywhere · tabular numerals · every chart has tooltips · URL holds the full view state · works in dark/light and on mobile. Hand-written SVG charts in vanilla ES modules, no runtime dependencies.
