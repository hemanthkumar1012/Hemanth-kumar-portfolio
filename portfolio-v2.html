03 MY APPROACH

Simple process.
Thoughtful outcome.

A practical loop for turning a vague idea into something people can try, understand, and use.

STEP 01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the why.

Clarify the user, the problem, and what a genuinely useful outcome should look like.

STEP 02 / BUILD

Make it real.

Choose the simplest sensible architecture, connect the pieces, and make a working first version.

STEP 03 / REFINE

Make it better.

Test the edges, simplify the interaction, and iterate until the whole experience feels coherent.
