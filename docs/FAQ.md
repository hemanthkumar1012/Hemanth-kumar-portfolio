# FAQ

**Q: Why pure HTML/CSS/JS?**  
A: Fastest load, zero build complexity, easy to host anywhere, full control over design.

**Q: How do I update projects?**  
A: Edit `projects.json` and the corresponding cards inside `portfolio-v2.html`.

**Q: Can I reuse the design?**  
A: Yes under MIT — keep attribution appreciated.

**Q: Dark mode?**  
A: On the roadmap. Current palette is a warm paper + forest green system.
